The studies behind it

Plain-English summaries, a link to the original, and the limits stated honestly. Small studies and models are leads, not proof.

Spine shape

Classification of the normal variation in the sagittal alignment of the human lumbar spine and pelvis in the standing position Roussouly et al. · Spine · 2005 The paper behind the four spine types. Types are set by sacral slope, measured on X-ray.
Classification of normal sagittal spine alignment: refounding the Roussouly classification Laouissat, Sebaaly, Gehrchen & Roussouly · European Spine Journal · 2018 · 296 adults Adds a fifth shape, Type 3 anteverted (16%): an S-curve with a forward-tipped pelvis and deep lower-back curve. Type 2 averaged a pelvic incidence of 41°, close to Type 1 (39°).
Biomechanical responses of human lumbar spine and pelvis according to the Roussouly classification Wang et al. · PLOS ONE · 2022 Computer models of each type: the Type 3 model was most stable; the straighter Type 1 and 2 models balanced and bore load less well. Models, not people.

Disc load and flat backs

The myth of lumbar instability: the importance of abnormal loading as a cause of low back pain Mulholland · European Spine Journal · 2008 Argues back pain comes from uneven load through a worn disc, not excess movement, and is felt in held postures. A surgeon’s argued review, not a trial.
Low lordosis is a common finding in young lumbar disc herniation patients J Exp Orthop · 2020 · 16 patients aged 14–24 Half were Type 2 and a quarter Type 1. Very small study; a link, not a cause.
Different spinal subtypes with varying characteristics of lumbar disc degeneration at specific level with age J Orthop Surg Res · 2020 · 209 adults with no back pain Each spine type showed its own pattern of disc wear with age; in Type 2 it stood out at L1–L3. Compares age groups within each type, not types against each other.

Running

The evolution and function of human lumbar lordosis variability Castillo · Harvard PhD thesis · 2017 Lower-back curve had no effect on shock absorption when walking, but a strong effect when running. A thesis, not a peer-reviewed paper.
Effects of step rate manipulation on joint mechanics during running Heiderscheit et al. · Med Sci Sports Exerc · 2011 · 45 runners Raising step rate 5–10% reduced vertical bounce and the energy absorbed at the knee. Healthy runners, single session.
Altering cadence or vertical oscillation during running: effects on running related injury factors Adams et al. · Int J Sports Phys Ther · 2018 · 20 runners A “less bounce” cue lowered loading rates and peak impact force more than a cadence cue did. Single session.
The effect of trunk flexion angle on lower limb mechanics during running Warrener, Tamai & Lieberman · Human Movement Science · 2021 · 23 runners More forward trunk bend: +29% loading rate, +20% impact peak, +70% hip extensor moment.
Biomechanical consequences of running with deep core muscle weakness Raabe & Chaudhari · Journal of Biomechanics · 2018 · model from 8 runners Weakening the deep core in a simulation raised lower-spine loading by up to about 15%. A computer model, not a trial.
The metabolic cost of human running: is swinging the arms worth it? Arellano & Kram · J Exp Biol · 2014 · 13 runners Running with the arms held still cost 3–13% more energy and increased torso rotation.
Relation of anterior pelvic tilt during running to clinical and kinematic measures of hip extension Schache et al. · Br J Sports Med · 2000 · 14 runners Less hip extension went with more forward pelvic tilt when running. A correlation, not cause.

Sitting

Posture of the trunk when sitting on forward inclining seats Bendix & Biering-Sørensen · Scand J Rehabil Med · 1983 Forward seat tilt increased the lower-back curve, about two-thirds of it from the hips. Small study, healthy subjects.
Biomechanical effects of sitting with adjustable ischial and lumbar support on occupational low back pain Makhsous et al. · BMC Musculoskeletal Disorders · 2009 · 35 people A rear seat that drops away cut lower-back muscle activity 6–24% and moved seat load forward onto the thighs. Flat backs not tested.
Where the sciatic nerve runs between the sit bone and the hip (cadaver study) Ma et al. · 2024 · 31 cadavers The nerve runs through the middle third of the gap between the sit bone and the outer hip bone.
A chair with a flexible four-segment seat pan Park et al. · Applied Sciences · 2025 Better comfort and less back-muscle fatigue, in healthy students only. Two authors work for the chair’s makers.
Chairs for low back pain (summary of Andersson 1979, Snijders 2004, Van Veelen 2000) WikiMSK summary Sitting reduces the lower-back curve; support low at L5 prevents the slump hinge; leave shoulder space above the lumbar support.
Intradiscal pressure in daily life (Wilke et al. 1999, summary) One subject Slouching gave the lowest disc pressure; sitting up straight actively raised it by about 10%. One person.
